<h2>About the share market today page</h2>
<p>This page brings together what moved the Indian stock market today: the closing values of the Nifty 50, Sensex, Bank Nifty and every NSE sectoral and thematic index; foreign (FII/FPI) and domestic (DII) institutional flows in the cash market; index option data such as the put-call ratio, max pain and open-interest levels; global indices from the US, Europe and Asia; crude oil, gold, silver and base metals; the rupee against the dollar and other currencies; and US Treasury and Indian government bond markets.</p>
<p>A short <b>morning note</b> (8:15 AM IST) summarises overnight global cues, and an <b>evening wrap</b> (7:30 PM IST) summarises the Indian session, breadth, sectors, flows and the next day's corporate actions. Both are written automatically from the data and contain no recommendations.</p>
<p>To find individual stocks, use the <a href="https://www.mnclgroup.com/candlestick-patterns">MNCL stock screener</a>: daily technical screens, open-interest build-up, corporate actions, candlestick patterns and charts.</p>
